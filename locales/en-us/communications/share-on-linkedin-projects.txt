Software Engineering Guide

Summary: Software Engineering Guide is an online guidebook of practical engineering practices for developer teams, covering people, architecture, security, data, delivery, and operations. The project contains years of experience working with many teams. The guidebook stays practical rather than theoretical, favoring the trade-offs real teams actually face over tidy abstract ideals.

Benefits: This guide gathers settled, widely accepted practice into one place, written in plain language, so a team lead preparing a proposal or a new engineer ramping up has a single reference worth trusting and pointing others to.

Please share: Please send this along to teams considering improvements, to a new engineers building their mental model of the field, and to colleagues who say someone should really write this down. A message to the right person, or a link dropped in a team channel, both help it reach the people who would actually use it.

Advice welcome: Help me improve this by sending your suggestions, feedback, ideas for improvements, and new concepts. Your help with this in turn helps many more people using this project.
