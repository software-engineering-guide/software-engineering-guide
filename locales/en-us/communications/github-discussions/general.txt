Say hello and tell us how you use Software Engineering Guide

Welcome to the Software Engineering Guide Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the guide.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Software Engineering Guide because I wanted a guidebook of good practices for software developer teams, including startups, enterprises, and government organizations to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which software engineering practice has made the biggest difference for your team?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
